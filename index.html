      How to use at a match

      1. Install it. Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen, works offline at the venue and keeps its storage.
      2. Mount the iPhone Duo unfolded on the tripod behind the glass, rear camera on the field, inner screen facing the drive team. The camera sits on one half of the fold, the driver HUD on the other (Layout swaps sides or goes full-screen HUD).
      3. Before the match: Match setup (number, alliance, partners, driver note), run the checklist, use the 0.5× lens / zoom to frame the whole field.
      4. Tap START MATCH on the AUTO start sound. Recording starts automatically. Use −1s / +1s if the field clock is off.
      5. Tap TELEOP GO when TELEOP starts (or let it auto-start after the pause), then tap who scored more FUEL in AUTO. The Driver Station game data shows it: R or B = that alliance's hub goes OFF first.
      6. Listen. 3-2-1 beeps before every change of our hub, voice callouts for each shift, END GAME and the climb call.
      7. Optional scouting: Scout opens over the camera (recording continues): tap FUEL, AUTO tower, climbs, and tag moments to jump to later.
      8. After 0:00 it keeps recording a few seconds, saves, and asks for the result. Library → Save to Photos to keep the video.

      Long-press the hub box to clear a wrong AUTO winner. Menu → Open driver HUD window opens a second synced view.
